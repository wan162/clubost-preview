Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

This website is run by Matrix Club Verwaltungsgesellschaft mbH, the operator of OST. We keep data collection to what the site needs. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and no tracking pixels, and embeds no social media. Fonts, images and scripts are served from our own server. This page explains what is processed anyway, why, and what your rights are.

1. Controller

Matrix Club Verwaltungsgesellschaft mbH
Warschauer Platz 18, 10245 Berlin, Germany
Phone 030 / 29369990
Email ahoi@ost-hafen.berlin
Represented by the managing director Dr. Ulrich G. Buhmann.

For every question about your data and for exercising your rights, write to the email address above.

2. Visiting the site: server logs

When you open a page, the web server records technical data about the request: your IP address, date and time, the page or file requested, the referring page if your browser sends one, your browser and operating system (user agent), the amount of data transferred and the status of the request. Nothing is stored in your browser.

  • Purpose: delivering the site correctly, keeping it secure and stable, recognising attacks and errors.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in a working and secure website.
  • Retention: log files are deleted after 14 days at the latest, unless a specific incident requires keeping them for its investigation.
  • Hosting: the site runs on servers in Germany operated by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, on the basis of a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR.

3. Cookies, analytics, third-party content

None. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking services. No content is loaded from third-party servers while you browse, which is why there is no cookie banner.

4. Contact forms

The site has one form with four subjects: bookings, press and general contact, jobs, and lost items. Depending on the subject it asks for your name, email address, an optional phone number and your message, plus the details needed for that subject: links to your music, a date, the size and kind of an event, the position you are applying for, or the night you lost something. Your message is sent to the mailbox or internal booking system of the team that handles that subject. Both run on servers in Germany. Only the people who deal with your request see it.

  • Purpose: answering and handling your request.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request aims at a contract (bookings, venue hire, applications) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for all other requests, our legitimate interest in answering people who write to us. Where you tick the consent box, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR applies in addition; you can withdraw that consent at any time with effect for the future.
  • Retention: requests that do not lead to a contract are deleted once they are settled, at the latest after twelve months. Correspondence that becomes part of a contract is kept for the statutory commercial and tax retention periods of up to ten years.

The same applies when you write to us directly by email, for example to lost@clubost.de. Ordinary email is not encrypted end to end; do not send us sensitive information by email.

5. Job applications

Applications sent through the jobs form or by email are processed to decide on the employment relationship (§ 26 BDSG, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Application documents are deleted six months after the end of the procedure at the latest, unless we conclude a contract with you or you agree to a longer storage.

6. Tickets

Tickets for our nights are sold by Resident Advisor. The link on this site takes you to their website; the purchase and its data are handled by Resident Advisor under their own privacy policy. For entry we receive from Resident Advisor the ticket data needed to check tickets at the door, which we process under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and delete after the night and the statutory retention periods.

7. Newsletter

On the start page you can sign up for our newsletter: programme, pre-sales and the occasional announcement, one or two mails a month. From time to time it also carries offers for subscribers, such as discounted tickets, community tickets and guest list spots. These go to everyone on the list by email; we build no profile of you and keep no points account for them. We ask for your email address; a first name is optional. The sign-up is double opt-in: after you send the form we mail you a confirmation link, and only once you click it is your address on the list. Without the click nothing is sent, and the sign-up is deleted after 30 days.

  • What we store: your email address, the first name if you gave one, the consent text you ticked, the date and time of the sign-up and of the confirmation, the IP address and browser used for each of those two steps, and the page you signed up on.
  • Purpose: sending you the newsletter, including the offers for subscribers described above, and being able to show that you asked for it.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, your consent, for the newsletter itself; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in being able to prove that consent, for the sign-up record. Advertising by email also needs your prior consent under § 7(2) no. 2 UWG, which the double opt-in documents.
  • Withdrawal: every newsletter and every mail about your subscription contains an unsubscribe link, and you can also write to the address in section 1. Withdrawing takes effect immediately for the future; you get no further newsletters.
  • Retention: as long as you are subscribed. After you unsubscribe we keep the record of your sign-up, your consent and your withdrawal for three years, the regular limitation period, so we can show that the mails we sent you were lawful; then it is deleted.
  • Where: the list is kept in our own club management system on servers in Germany (Hetzner Online GmbH, see section 2). The confirmation mails go out through our email provider, who acts as our processor. We pass the list to nobody else. Should we use a newsletter service to send the mails, we will name it here first.
  • No tracking: the mails from our system contain no tracking pixels and no personalised links other than the unsubscribe link, so we do not see whether you opened a mail.

8. External links

Links to Resident Advisor, Instagram, Facebook, Linktree and Google Maps are plain links. Nothing is loaded from these services until you click. Once you do, you are on their sites and their privacy policies apply. We have no influence on what they process.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15), to have it rectified (Art. 16) or erased (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on our legitimate interests (Art. 21). Where processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future. To exercise any of these rights, write to ahoi@ost-hafen.berlin.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59-61, 10555 Berlin, datenschutz-berlin.de.

10. No automated decisions

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

11. Changes

We update this page when the site changes. The date at the top shows the current version.
