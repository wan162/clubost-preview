    OST is a techno club in the former power station of the Berlin Osthafen, on the Spree between Friedrichshain and Treptow. High brick walls on the outside. Inside: two floors, the main floor and the Lager.

    Line-ups change. The room, the sound and the people are what you come back for. Leave the week at the door, let go for a few hours and look after the person next to you.

    The building

    1. 1907

      The city of Berlin starts building the Osthafen on the Spree, led by Stadtbaurat Friedrich Krause. A modern industrial port with iron halls, electric cranes and its own power supply.

    2. 1913

      The port opens on 28 September. Its power station stands at the edge of the complex, on the border to the old fishing village of Stralau. The Berlin bear sits above the gate. The gables of the machine hall follow the iron trusses inside.

    3. 1993

      The building is restored. For the next twelve years it houses the offices of a newspaper. The hall stays silent.

    4. Now

      The Osthafen is a listed monument. The halls belong to OST. Where the generators once stood, there is a dance floor, and the walls have a few more stories to carry.

    Our own nights

    OST Klubnacht, Convent, Ultra Sozial by THAM, ODD, Sektor B, Blasphemy and Mayhem are made in-house. Every Thursday is Savory: one floor, free entry till 00:30, the night Berlin locals come for.

    In between, the promoters we trust bring their crowds: Euphorik, Mixmag, Devoted, Onyx, Verknipt, Revolver, Polyamor, Slave to the Rave, Pinky Promise, Gabber Industries, Sesh, Gegen.

    House rules

    OST is for everyone who respects that. Taking care of each other is what makes the night. If something is wrong, go to the bar or the door and we will help.

    No photos, no videos inside. Your camera gets a sticker at the door and the phone stays in your pocket. What happens on the floor stays there.

    Every bar is cashless. Bring a card or your phone.

    Good to know

    Door

    Our door team decides who gets in. Know which night you are going to, come in small groups, be respectful, and do not show up wasted. Nobody gets in under 18, so bring photo ID.

    Tickets

    Advance tickets are sold on Resident Advisor; the links are in the programme. Some nights sell out. Thursdays are free till 00:30 and have no advance tickets.

    Getting there

    Alt-Stralau 1, 10245 Berlin, at the tip of the Stralau peninsula. From S Treptower Park cross the Elsenbrücke, from S Ostkreuz walk along the Spree. Both take about 15 minutes on foot. Taxis and ride shares drop you at the gate.
